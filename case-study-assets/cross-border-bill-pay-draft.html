01Summary

Migrants and expats send ~30% of their remittances toward bills back home — through indirect, fragmented processes. I led the UX strategy and design for Western Union's first direct Cross-Border Bill Pay experience, replacing a legacy, low-adoption product (Quick Pay) with a modern, biller-network-backed flow — piloting in the E1 Canada app with a clear path to scale globally.

02My Role

Design Strategist & Lead — owned the end-to-end UX strategy and design leadership: framed the opportunity, defined the vision narrative and guiding principles, led the competitive analysis, designed the core flows and high-fidelity prototype, and partnered with Product, partner-integration, Compliance, and Engineering to shape the phased roadmap.

03The Problem

  • Senders had no way to pay a bill directly — they had to trust receivers to complete payment after funds landed.
  • Receivers relied on manual, cash-based, or fragmented local processes, often missing due dates.
  • Neither side had visibility into whether or when a bill was actually paid — low transparency, low trust.
  • A prior attempt (Quick Pay) failed to scale due to a cumbersome biller-setup process and weak UX.

Opportunity: ~$170B in addressable transaction volume globally, ~$25–30M in annual revenue potential — concentrated in corridors like US→Mexico, US→India, and UAE→Philippines.

03bBusiness Goals

From the presentation deck

  • Capture a $25–$30M annual revenue opportunity (2-year projection)
  • Position Western Union as a market leader in cross-border bill pay
  • Deliver a secure & transparent, end-to-end bill pay experience
  • Position bill pay as a high-frequency, essential service — not a one-off transaction
  • Build a scalable, global bill pay capability
  • Drive organic revenue growth

04My Approach

1
Reframed the problem as a strategist
Treated bill pay as its own first-class journey — "pay the bill directly," not "send money and hope" — anchoring every downstream design decision.
2
Competitive & experience benchmarking
Teardown across global players (Wise, Xoom by PayPal, Careem Pay, Comera Pay) and Canada-specific products (PaySimply, Beacon) to define "best-in-class": global biller coverage, a simple/fast/trusted experience, low cost & risk, localized payment options, simpler biller onboarding, robust reconciliation, and compliance built into the flow.
3
Grounded the vision in real personas
Built 5 scenario narratives (migrant worker, student abroad, supporting sibling, busy professional, underbanked receiver) to pressure-test design against real friction.
4
Defined 4 design principles for a scalable platform
Radical transparency · Purpose-built flows · Sender–receiver collaboration · Built to scale, tailored locally — used as the north star for engineering and compliance throughout build.
5
Designed the core end-to-end flow
Led wireframes and a high-fidelity prototype across two journeys — the first-time user (never paid via WU) and the returning user (has paid before) — covering biller selection, bill discovery & linking, transparent fee/FX breakdown, localized pay-in, and confirmation/reminders.
6
Partnered across the org
Worked with Product on objectives/hypotheses, partner teams on biller-catalog integration, Compliance on corridor/biller whitelisting, and Engineering on a phased delivery roadmap.

05Key Design Decisions

  • Transparency-first checkout: every screen shows bill due amount, FX rate, fee breakdown, and total before commit.
  • Progressive biller discovery: search/filter by country, category, and name — scales to thousands of billers.
  • Two tailored onboarding paths: distinct flows for first-time vs. returning users, so repeat payers skip steps they've already done.
  • Design for the underbanked, not just digital-native: OCR "photograph your bill" path for less tech-savvy users.
  • Compliance embedded, not bolted on: corridors/biller types filtered at the front end per Risk & Compliance rules — invisible to users, critical to architecture.

05bPhased Roadmap

From the presentation deck

Phase 1 — Baseline experience

  • Pay a new bill, for new & existing users
  • Bill Pay homepage; bill search, selection, fetch & pay (domestic & cross-border)
  • Transaction history, track a transfer, FAQs, loyalty program

Phase 2 — Advanced bill management

  • Auto-fetch new bills for existing users based on prior payment details, with proactive notification
  • AutoPay — invoke users to set up recurring payments
  • Gmail/SMS read access (one-time consent) to auto-fetch bills from issuer emails
